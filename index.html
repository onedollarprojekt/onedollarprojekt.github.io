The idea

A small request.
A worldwide question.

The experiment asks whether someone would voluntarily send the equivalent of €1 to an anonymous project creator. There is no obligation, no pressure, and no promised reward.

◎

One simple request

The suggested amount is €1 or a clearly displayed equivalent in another currency. Choosing not to contribute is completely fine.

◇

Three possible outcomes

Confirmed contribution, explicit decline, or no reply within the stated observation period.

✦

Honest reporting

Only verified totals will be published. No invented participants, payments, replies, or country statistics.

How it works

Respect first.
Data second.

People should only receive an invitation through a channel where contact is permitted and they have agreed to receive it. No purchased address lists, unsolicited mass-email campaign, repeated reminders, or pressure tactics will be used.

The experiment will begin only after the contact method, payment handling, and privacy information have been reviewed and set up appropriately.

“A person's choice matters just as much as the result.”

This is an independent social experiment, not a charity, investment, or promise of financial return. Participation is optional.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I have to send €1?

No. Participation is entirely voluntary. You can decline or simply not take part.

Is this a charity or an investment?

No. It is an independent social experiment. Contributions are not investments and do not create a right to a return, reward, or service.

Is payment available now?

No. Payment collection is not enabled during this pre-launch phase. Any future payment method will be added only after the responsible account holder and applicable requirements have been reviewed.

How will results be counted?

Only confirmed payments and explicit replies will be counted. Non-responses will be counted only after a clearly stated observation period. Public results will be aggregated.

Who is behind the project?

The project creator is not publicly named on this page. The project will publish the legally required operator and privacy information before accepting payments or launching invitations.
